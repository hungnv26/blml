What you can do with it

Talk, properly

One-to-one or in a group. Typing indicators, delivery and read marks, replies, edits, and voice notes when typing is too slow.

React and reply

Hold any message for reactions, reply, forward, copy or pin — the gestures you already know.

Share real files

Photos, videos, documents and audio up to 100 MB each — a real video, not a compressed smear of one.

Call each other

Voice and video calls, relayed through our own server when the network won't let devices talk directly.

Keep a group organised

Mentions, pinned messages and polls, so "what are we eating" takes one tap each instead of forty messages.

Find each other easily

Scan a QR code, add by phone number or email, or pick from people already in your groups.
